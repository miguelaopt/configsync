# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions — is expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask before judging.
- Welcome newcomers; nobody is born knowing Drizzle.
- Keep it on topic and free of harassment, discrimination, sexualised content, personal attacks and doxxing.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that don't follow this, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Report problems privately to the maintainers at **miguel.rf267@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
